1

Install on your PC.

Download Rovarin for Windows and follow setup. Keep the PIN it creates for you.

2

3

Scan the QR code in Rovarin or open its mobile address. Enter your PIN and you’re in.

Staying at your desk?

Local use works without Tailscale. For phone access, your PC needs to be on and connected. No router port forwarding.

How access works ↗

Good to know

A few quick answers.

Is Rovarin free?

Yes, for personal and noncommercial use. The code is available under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0.

Do I need Tailscale?

Only to access Rovarin from another device. You can use the Windows desktop app locally without it. Tailscale is not bundled with the installer.

Is it ready for everyday use?

Rovarin is an Experimental Alpha, so expect some rough edges. Hardware support varies, and optional sensors may be unavailable. Read the release notes before installing.

Why might Windows show an installer warning?

The installer is unsigned. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning. Our download page explains how to check the installer without disabling Windows security.
